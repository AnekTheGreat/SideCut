## 83.0.1 ({{STAMP}}): Other apps under the gear, the whole family in one place

- **WHAT THE OWNER SAID.** *"where's the stuff in SideCut the other apps part in settings before settings and more"*, then, on hearing it was not there at all: *"add it bro and v83.0.1"*.
- **WHAT WAS MISSING.** FamTM and UJournal both carry an Other apps tab; SideCut carried none of the names. A search of the whole page for `FamTM`, `UJournal`, `MeasureT` and `My Hub` came back empty - not in Settings, not in Support, nowhere. The app a person is most likely to open first was the one that never mentioned the rest.
- **THE ANSWER.** A ninth tab in `#settingsTabStrip`, last before More, carrying the same inline style as its neighbours so the strip still reads as one row: Theme, Donate, Important, Glow, Sandbox, Support, Widget, Other apps, More. `#settingsPaneOther` is a sibling of the other panes at the top of `#settingsPanesWrap`, holding four cards in the shape the Support tab already uses for its contact block - a name, the line that says what the app does, and a link.
- **THE LIST IS THE ONE THE OTHER TWO CARRY.** FamTM (`com.FamTM.app`), UJournal (`app.ujournal`), MeasureT (`com.CompassEDC.myapp`) and My Hub (`AnekTheGreat/My-Hub`, which is not on Play) - the same four, the same one-line descriptions, the same destinations as `APPS` in FamTM and the list in UJournal, so the family does not read differently depending on which app was opened.
- **THE THREE WIRES EVERY TAB NEEDS, AND NOTHING ELSE.** `showSettingsTab` gets the pane's `display` line beside the other eight, the `active` toggle beside the other eight, and the tab its own click listener beside `settingsTabMore`'s. `scTabIntoView` and the arrival animation both build their element id from the tab name, so `other` reaches `settingsTabOther` and `settingsPaneOther` with no further wiring. No quick action, no Home bubble and no AI prompt entry was touched: this is a tab in Settings, not a new destination.
- **WHAT WAS CHECKED.** `python3 dev/patch-8301.py --check` exited **1** before the payload and **0** after it; a second run printed "already on the page" and left the page byte for byte identical (`cmp`). Every inline script in the page compiles. `node dev/check-8301.cjs` loads the patched page in jsdom and clicks the tab: nine tabs with Other apps directly before More, every older tab still present, the pane hidden before the tap and the only pane shown after it, four cards naming FamTM, UJournal, MeasureT and My Hub with their four links, the tab wearing `active` while More does not, and the page saying 83.0.1. {{ROOT}} bytes on the full channel and {{PLAY}} on the store build; both OTA bundles reached their fixed point in the release commit.
- **NOT CLAIMED.** No phone ran here: the tab was driven in jsdom, not tapped on a device, and no card was followed through Android's WebView out to a browser. What is proven is the markup the release ships, the routing that shows the pane, and that the four cards are the four apps pointing at their four pages.
