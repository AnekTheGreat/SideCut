## 83.0.8 ({{STAMP}}): the widget only ever plays SideCut

- **WHAT THE OWNER SAID.** *"now it. plays stupid Spotify audio"*, after installing 83.0.7. A press on SideCut's widget started Spotify.
- **WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG, AND IT WAS MINE.** When the press was handed back to SideCut it was not addressed to SideCut at all: it went out as `AudioManager.dispatchMediaKeyEvent(...)`, a system-wide media key. Android delivers that to whichever player it considers active - with SideCut dead, that is whatever played last, and here that was Spotify. And the "did it work?" test was `isMusicActive()`, which answers for the *whole phone*: Spotify started, the phone said "yes, music is playing", the hidden player was never started, and SideCut's widget had launched somebody else's app.
- **THE FIX, IN THREE PARTS.** (1) A press is no longer handed back unless SideCut itself is running: the heartbeat window drops from 90 s to 15 s, so a corpse stops looking alive. (2) The hand-back now counts as success only when SideCut's own heartbeat **moves** in the next 2.5 s - no other app can produce that, so no other app can masquerade as success. (3) With nothing of SideCut's running, the press goes straight to the hidden player and **no media key is sent at all**, so there is no way left for a press to reach another player.
- **THE HIDDEN PLAYER STOPS CREDITING STRANGERS.** Its watchdog used to accept `isMusicActive()` as proof of its own sound; it now accepts the page's own report, or music *plus* a heartbeat SideCut is still writing. Silence from another app can no longer be mistaken for success, and the player can no longer "give up" over music it is actually playing.
- **WHAT WAS CHECKED.** `python3 dev/patch-8308.py --check` exits **1** before the payload and **0** after it, a second run is a no-op, the page lands at 83.0.8 with a new cache name and changelog entry, and all seven inline scripts still parse under `node --check`. The native chain was run end to end on a sandbox Android project - `patch-widget.py`, `dev/widget-sizes.py`, `dev/ghost-host.py` - and the generated `GhostHostActivity` now has exactly three exits: switch off hands back, SideCut running hands back and is verified by its heartbeat, and everything else starts the hidden player. Balanced braces, a well-formed manifest declaring both activities, and an identical tree on a second run. The rebuilt bundles reached their fixed point in the release commit: {{ROOT}} bytes on the full channel and {{PLAY}} on the store build.
- **NOT CLAIMED.** No phone ran this. What is proven from the code: the media-key path is unreachable unless SideCut's heartbeat is under 15 s old, and it cannot be counted as success by another app. What is still a device question: whether an alpha-0 window keeps WebView audio alive at all - if it does not, the shade will now say "player -> no audio, opening the app" and the trail will name that step.
