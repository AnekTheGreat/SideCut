## 83.3.0 ({{STAMP}}): The headphones wake the sound again

- **WHAT WAS STILL WRONG.** 83.2.9 gave the swipe-away player a session. The page had a quieter fault of its own: every deck song's sound leaves through the Web Audio graph (normalisation, EQ, boost, the limiter), and an element inside a graph sends its audio there and nowhere else. An AudioContext the phone had put to sleep therefore turned a healthy `play()` into silence - the card said playing, the playhead moved, and nothing came out. That is what a press from the earbuds "not unpausing" looked like.
- **THE FIX.** The press wakes the mixer before it starts the song (`scAudioCtxAwake`), a parked song wakes it once a minute so it is ready (`startParkedBeat`), and every press from outside goes through one door (`scTransportPlay`, dev/8330-drive.js.txt) that starts the song, retries once through `recoverAudio`, and reports what happened.
- **AND IT SAYS WHERE IT STOPPED.** A press that cannot put sound in the air prints one short line on screen and writes the same line into Settings -> Widget, in the trail the widget work already keeps: no player, a refusal named (`NotAllowedError` and friends), or a mixer that will not wake. Silence stops being indistinguishable from a press that never arrived.
- **CHECKS.** `dev/patch-8330.py --check` exits 1 before the payload and 0 after it, a second run is a no-op, all seven inline scripts of the patched page parse, and the door is driven in the session that wrote it against a fake element and a fake context: a sleeping mixer is woken before `play()`, a refusal is spoken and retried through `recoverAudio`, the restore path takes an empty element, and a missing player is reported. Rebuilt bundles: {{ROOT}} bytes on the full channel, {{PLAY}} on the store build.
- **NOT CLAIMED.** No phone and no earbuds ran here: nothing was listened to and no headset button was pressed. What is proven is the order the press performs its steps in, on the bytes the release ships. If a press still comes out silent, the line it now prints names the step that refused.
