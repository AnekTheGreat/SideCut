# Patch notes

The in-app notes are the source of truth: an installed copy shows them under Settings → What's new,
taken from the `CHANGELOG` in `index.html`, and a release is not cut without an entry at its head.
This file mirrors the newest release for anyone reading the repository, and `FIX_NOTES.md` holds the
long form of a fix — what was wrong, why it was wrong, and what changed.

## 81.5

- Moving around is fixed: Library opens on the first tap from Studio or Tools, and the cloud layout spaces its bubbles and its top bar so nothing sits on top of anything else.
- Cropping is done by ear: play the song inside the crop sheet, drop the playhead anywhere in it, and set the start or the end at the second you are hearing.
- Studio has DJ sets: name a set, add songs, reorder them or sort them by tempo, choose the blend, then play it or keep it as a playlist.
