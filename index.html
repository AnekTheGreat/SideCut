Where to get music
SideCut doesn't download music for you — it plays files you already have. To get songs, open your playlist, album, or track in Spotify, tap Share → Copy link, then paste it into a Spotify-to-MP3 downloader like Spotidown (spotidown.com) or any other downloader. Once you have the MP3 files, tap + Files in SideCut and import them — they'll be stored locally on your device.
Shown automatically the first time you open the app — replay it anytime from here.
The whole tutorial in text:
•Getting music in: SideCut plays audio files saved on your device. Tap + Files for individual songs or + Add folder for a whole folder. Import library restores a backup .zip.
•Playing: tap any song to play it; use the mini player at the bottom for play/pause, skip, and shuffle. Tap the ⋮ next to a song for more options.
•Jump to the playing song: tap the record on the mini player — it opens the full now-playing view and scrolls straight to that song. No need to hunt for it.
•Look: Settings → Theme for looks, Glow for edge lines and glow, Playback for crossfade/gapless, Equalizer for 8-band EQ.
•Organizing: create playlists from the Playlists tab, add songs via the ⋮ menu, long-press a row to select multiple tracks, and Export library to back everything up as a .zip.
•Notifications: the media player in your phone's notification shade plays/pauses but tapping it won't open SideCut — open the app manually to get back to the song list.

Enabled
Rolling back swaps the app to a saved copy of an older version that lives as a separate, self-contained snapshot on this device — the actual SideCut app installed on your device (and its cached shell) stays exactly as it is; only what this tab shows changes. Your library and settings live in their own storage, untouched by the switch.
⚠ Before you roll back
Older versions can have side effects: data loss or a broken app that won't open or run correctly. If that happens you'll need to clear app data to recover, which wipes your library. Back up your library first — use Export library to save a .zip you can restore after clearing data. Don't roll back without a backup.

Understanding the version numbers
Turn DJ Mode off for any individual playlist — handy for religious or devotional playlists you'd rather listen to as-is. Built-in names (Paath, Gurbani, Kirtaan, Dhadi Jhatta, Gurmukhi, Religious, Prayer) are always off and shown locked below.
A few built-in behaviors worth knowing:
•DJ Mode is disabled for certain playlists. If a playlist is named Paath, Gurbani, Kirtaan, Dhadi Jhatta, Gurmukhi, Religious, or Prayer — uppercase or lowercase, it doesn't matter — DJ Mode steps aside for that playlist. You can also turn DJ Mode off manually for any playlist from DJ Mode per playlist above — handy for religious or devotional playlists you'd rather listen to as-is. You'll see a small note when this happens.
•Your music stays on your device. SideCut plays files you import — it never uploads them. Fetching new lyrics or covers, and update checks, are the only things that need internet.
•Update timestamps are in Eastern Time. Every changelog entry shows its date and time in ET (EDT in summer, EST in winter) — never UTC.
•Jump to the playing song from anywhere. Tap the record on the mini player at the bottom — it opens the full now-playing view and scrolls straight to that song, so you never have to hunt for it in a playlist.
•The phone's media player can't open SideCut. The notification-shade / lock-screen player can play, pause, and skip — but tapping it won't open the app. To get back to the song list, open SideCut from your launcher.
•Lyrics auto-scroll isn't perfect. Timed lyrics are matched to a best-effort sync, so the highlight can drift — tap Auto-scroll in the lyrics view to turn it off and read at your own pace.
•Back up before rolling back. Older app versions can break things or lose data. Use Export library to save a .zip first, since recovering from a bad rollback may require clearing app data.

How to use SideCut

Getting music into your library
SideCut plays audio files already saved on your device — it doesn't stream or fetch anything itself. One way to get songs onto SideCut is via Spotify. Click onto your playlist, Click share, then copy the link. After that go to Spotidown (spotidown.com) or any other Spotify to mp3 downloader. after that click files and select all of your songs and import them. Now you have all of your songs locally stored right on yout device in one specific setting.
•Tap + Files to pick one or more individual songs
•Tap + Add folder to grab a whole folder at once — handy for a full album or a Downloads folder
•Use Import library to restore a full backup .zip, including playlists and tags
Playing music
•Tap any song in your list to start playing it
•Use the mini player at the bottom for play/pause, skip, and shuffle
•Tap the record on the mini player to open the full now-playing view and jump straight to the song that's playing — no scrolling needed
•Tap the ⋮ (kebab) next to a song for more options — add to playlist, play next, delete, and more
Customizing the look
•Open Settings (gear icon) → Theme to pick a look, or build your own custom theme
•Under Settings → Glow, mix and match edge lines, blur bands, and corner glow — all independent toggles, all customizable
•Settings → Playback has crossfade, gapless mode, and normalization
•Settings → Equalizer has 8 adjustable bands plus presets
Playlists & organizing
•Create playlists from the Playlists tab, then add songs via the kebab menu on any track
•Long-press a song row to select multiple tracks at once
•Export library anytime to back everything up as a .zip you can restore later
